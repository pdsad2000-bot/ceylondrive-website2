Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a quote take?

Typically under 5 minutes through our website.

Do you cover flood damage?

Yes, our Premium All-Risk plan covers natural perils including floods.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes, we offer monthly payment plans through major banks in Sri Lanka.

What is No-Claim Bonus (NCB)?

A discount on your premium if you don't make any claims for a year.

Is roadside assistance free?

It is free for Standard and Premium plan holders island-wide.

How do I cancel my policy?

You can notify us via email 14 days in advance for a refund.
